# Owner-only DEMO trade recovery pressure observer — 20 November 2026

## Objective

Investigate the owner's observed large givebacks, including November 20 USDCAD shorts that temporarily recorded favorable P&L but closed negative after commissions. In the audited Pepperstone USD DEMO ledger, a USDCAD SELL opened 15:08:24 UTC showed max favorable gross P&L $64.00 and closed 15:18:27 UTC at gross -$184.68, commission -$29.40, **broker net -$214.08**. A distinct USDCAD SELL opened 14:51:22 UTC had a +$50.31 recorded peak and closed with gross -$177.85 and commission -$29.40, **net -$207.25**. Neither validates that a different exit was possible, and these separate records do not conclusively identify the user's UI example (peak $60.85).

The user's request is to monitor whether a position facing reversal may fail to recover within the next **5, 10, 15 or 20 minutes** without undermining the profitable positions that currently reach their targets. This change **does NOT introduce a new exit strategy**, broker stop amendment or automatic trigger into current profit protection.

## New active prospective observer

- Pure as-of scoring and outcome functions: `apps/api/scripts/owner-demo-recovery-risk-shadow.cjs`.
- Read-only monitor: `apps/api/scripts/watch-owner-demo-recovery-risk-shadow.cjs`.
- Isolated PM2 config: `apps/api/scripts/irexpro-owner-recovery-risk-shadow.pm2.config.cjs`.
- Scope: one exact pre-configured owner account, one Pepperstone cTrader broker connection, account type DEMO and currency USD. Eligible original routes are EARLY_TRANSITION and CONFIRMED_REVERSAL. **No LIVE data or control.**
- Activation marker, observer state and date-stamped append-only observations in `/home/lightworld/webapps/irexpro-research/recovery-risk-forward`; summary in `latest-summary.json`.
- Enrollment: only when the trade is **actually OPEN**, opened after activation, and seen within **90 seconds** of broker opening. No hindsight reconstruction of closed trade snapshots.
- Frequency: polling about every 12 seconds for **new, on-time** OPEN trade snapshots at trade ages 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 18, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, and 60 minutes. A snapshot must be taken in its first 30 seconds; otherwise it is missed, not backfilled. This provides early warnings for positions that might close before minute 5.
- Each snapshot records a separate **5/10/15/20-minute** recovery-pressure *scenario*, rather than a fabricated percent probability that a losing trade will or will not recover. Labels are currently observational, untrained and uncalibrated.
- Uses completed positive-spread broker-native M1 quote observations from the trade's exact broker connection, with recorded timestamps, positive-quote counts and updated-at no later than snapshot time. EURUSD candles with zero spread and inadequate positive samples are **rejected**. Uses the same close side as the actual BUY/SELL exit for quote-only gross P&L.
- Uses original immutable intent stop/fill/lot size for risk geometry, 1/3/5-minute direction-adjusted price momentum and any *fresh* as-of observed broker P&L peak/giveback. Estimated fee reserve is accepted only if supported by a pre-fill allocation's `historicalFeePerLot`; zero or missing fee coverage produces no hypothetical after-fee quote figure.
- Scenario categories: `TOWARD_ORIGINAL_STOP` where adverse near-stop momentum is observed, `POSITIVE_PEAK_GIVEBACK_WITH_COUNTER_MOMENTUM` where a fee-covered peak has been lost amid confirmed 1/3-minute counter-momentum, `COUNTER_MOMENTUM_WATCH` and `NO_STRONG_REVERSAL_WARNING` for other valid conditions, plus explicit unknowns if quotes or entry data are invalid. **No labels claim a permanent or inevitable reversal.**
- After each snapshot, separately records eventual actual broker settlement when available, or the future *as-of* positive-spread quote-only mark at +5/10/15/20 minutes **only if still OPEN**. A position closed before the horizon gets its actual net settlement and close reason. Missing future checkpoints expire; no mutable historical quote backfill.
- Reports both high-risk warnings and **warned winning trades**, deduplicating warning settlements by trade ID to avoid inflating loss savings. Several independent days and warned/unwarned, net-of-commission comparisons are still required before training/calibrating probabilities or considering a discretionary change. Due to nonrandomized observational cohorts, an after-the-fact improvement cannot prove changing exits would have saved the same amount.

## Isolation, safety and deployment

The new process connects to PostgreSQL in `BEGIN ISOLATION LEVEL REPEATABLE READ READ ONLY`, queries the correct demo broker records and writes **only** the research directory. It does not call the cTrader trading API, issue broker orders, update models or alter existing profit protection, take profit, stop loss, lot size, margin gates, risk vetoes or LIVE account behavior. A watcher watchdog restarts this research process only on a >90-second stall or repeated errors. It is safe to stop independently if quote quality is insufficient.

The current model **cannot** reliably predict that a price "will never return". It first has to learn which *real-time* observed signs historically precede stop-loss-like exits and distinguish them from normal retracements on eventual winners. User-visible results must distinguish recorded marked favorable peaks, theoretical quote-only after-fee values, actual broker fills, and provider-native stop-trigger causes (not yet verified in the database).

**Rollout rule:** collect forward proof from multiple untouched trading days, compare warnings *against eventual winners as well as losers*, evaluate actual after-cost economics and broker quote coverage, and run shadow counterfactuals with realistic slippage/fees before any proposal to alter automatic protection. No automatic promotion is permitted.

## Forward-validation scorecard and strict pre-entry commission evidence (continued 20 Nov)

A second research-only scoreboard, `apps/api/scripts/owner-demo-recovery-risk-evaluation.cjs`, is embedded in `latest-summary.json` as `forwardValidation`. It separates Confirmed Reversal and Early Transition, and for each route distinguishes:
- **WARNED:** one or more prospectively captured near-stop/giveback warnings.
- **UNWARNED:** at least one valid, prospectively captured no-warning checkpoint.
- **INSUFFICIENT_OR_MISSING_EVIDENCE:** zero verified checkpoints or deficient real-time market evidence.

The scorecard counts **actual broker-settled winners, losers, signed net P&L and independent entry days once per trade**, not once per repeating warning or future horizon. For each 5/10/15/20-minute horizon it reports broker-closed outcomes versus valid quote-only marks versus unknown/missed data, including favorable/adverse sampled quote change from the original OPEN-time mark. Broker quote marks do NOT prove executable exit prices. It measures first-warning lead time against actual closure (>=10/30/60 seconds) to distinguish actionable lead time from hindsight warnings. It logs how many **winning positions would have been exposed to premature intervention**; there is no estimated saved-profit claim.

Manual calibration review is permitted in the *scorecard* only when each route has at least five distinct broker-settled entry days, >=20 warned settled positions and >=30 unwarned settled positions from >=3 days in each category. Even after that threshold the module still reports `sufficientStatisticalEvidence:false`, `modelIsCalibrated:false`, `noExecutionAuthority:true` and `autoPromote:false`; passing minimum counts is NOT proof of financial benefit.

**Additional critical fee data correction:** The first genuinely prospective USDCAD SELL opened 16:07:12 UTC and was scored at 16:08:22 UTC; the original sizing allocation did not contain `demoExecutionRiskEnvelope.historicalFeePerLot`, so the initial frozen risk snapshot correctly marked net-after-fees **unknown**. It must NEVER be retrospectively upgraded to a scored profit after that trade settles.

To ensure future entries have a defensible **AS-OF** cost reserve, `watch-owner-demo-recovery-risk-shadow.cjs` now queries the **same exact owner's same broker DEMO USD connection** for up to **32 earlier CLOSED trades strictly before the new trade's opened_at**, within seven days. No new trade's own future cost can contribute to its reserve. At 16:07:12 UTC, a separate read-only database check verified **32/32 previous trades had identical fully-booked commissions of $6.00 per standard lot**. If pre-fill capital allocation contains the verified fee, that takes precedence; otherwise the new shadow requires **12-32** previous settlements with positive, stable per-lot fees (max/min <=1.05, mean within observed bounds). For a subsequent 4.90-lot DEMO position, an independently verified historical 6.00 USD/lot would produce **$29.40 estimated round-trip broker fee**, not an actual new broker charge or a guarantee of future commission. Insufficient, missing or unstable evidence yields **unknown**, never an invented or zero fee.

The prior-sample source, sample count and reserved dollar amount are immutably saved at **OPEN-only enrollment**, within 90 seconds; prior data are queried inside a read-only transaction only for *new* prospective entries, preserving low VPS workload and avoiding any look-ahead. No existing trade snapshots are retroactively edited or reclassified. This is a research feature that improves later calibration of genuine **after-cost recovery risk**, not a broker instruction.

Verification: initial recovery monitor 11 tests; expanded forward evaluation and fee tests total 24 focused tests, all passing. Trading API/profit-protection processes are unchanged; separate recovery monitor restarts to load the enhanced report only.

**Historical fee timestamp hardening:** the read-only prior-settlement query additionally requires `created_at <= new_trade.opened_at` and `updated_at <= new_trade.opened_at`. That prevents a later broker reconciliation update from leaking its corrected fee backward into a historic forecast. A strict as-of owner-broker DB check for the USDCAD 16:07:12 UTC entry retained **32 prior settled samples at exactly $6.00 per lot**, even with these timestamp constraints. Any sample that fails provenance is excluded and fee remains unknown when coverage is inadequate.

### Separate fee-covered early-warning hypothesis

Added `earlyProfitFade()` to the **pure research model** while keeping existing severe `state` unchanged. At a verified *as-of* OPEN checkpoint, label `NET_PROFIT_FADING_M1_FIRST_TURN` only when ALL conditions hold: positive broker-native mark after a strictly pre-entry historical fee reserve; original stop headroom >0.35 initial stop-risk; timely recorded broker-mark profitable peak >1.5x fee reserve; gross quote mark has retained between 20% and 80% of recorded profitable gross peak; M1 direction-adjusted momentum has turned down at least 0.055R; M3 direction-adjusted momentum has **not yet** deteriorated by >=0.11R. No observed fees, stale native positive-spread quotes, absent path peaks, already-negative after-fee positions, or insufficient quote history => no early caution.

Every early caution explicitly says **watch only**, quote-not-fill, may recover, no predictive probabilities, and *no modification to profit-protection, account stop/TP, lot size, market orders, or risk gates*. Unlike a high-risk warning, these weak cautions are **never included in the automatic severe warning cohort**.

Added prospective and separate route-specific evaluation `earlyStillProfitableCautions` to `owner-demo-recovery-risk-evaluation.cjs`. Counts all distinct cautioned trades, actual after-cost winners (false-positive financial risk), actual losers, first-caution lead time to actual settlement >=30/60/120 seconds, quote-side cost-positive marks and repeated cautions without double-counting booked P&L. These are observable risk indicators, **not projected savings or recovered cash**. Minimum unreviewed future evidence remains mandatory before a probabilistic prediction model can be fitted or proposed as an automatic exit.

## 20 November — fast-loss coverage gap and independent sub-minute monitoring

A separate read-only **exact owner Pepperstone USD DEMO** audit of 302 actual settled trades opened from November 17 through the time of review showed: **72 broker settlements inside five minutes, 17 inside two minutes, two inside one minute**, one of those first-minute settlements a loser. A particularly important 7.00-lot USDJPY SELL opened 16:36:17 UTC and closed **16:36:57 UTC** via persisted `BROKER_CLOSE`, booked gross -$233.68, commission -$42.00, **net -$275.68**. Its recorded path favorable peak was $0.00, not a favorable-to-adverse giveback, and the existing first one-minute checkpoint was impossible after closure. These first-minute cases must **never** be silently counted as model misses or hypothetical profitable rescues; original broker SL trigger reason remains unverified without provider deal provenance.

To cover observability rather than fabricate hindsight, added `apps/api/scripts/owner-demo-subminute-recovery-shadow.cjs`, fully independent from entry selection and the existing mature 1/2/3/... minute recovery signals. It can **only** create native **OPEN-time forming-M1** quote observations in the 5–60 seconds after actual fill, if a post-activation trade was genuinely observed OPEN. The existing 12-second monitor cadence can yield multiple real-time observations within the first minute; polls that miss a fast closure simply cannot recover it later.

Every quote must come from the exact Pepperstone cTrader USD DEMO source `ctrader-broker-native`, exact instrument and broker connection, M1 **forming bar** with valid nonzero positive bid-ask spread <=3 pips, native positive sample count >=2, actual sampled timestamp **after the broker fill** and <=10 seconds before observer clock, and `created_at/updated_at` no later than the captured now. Missing or stale native quotes cause abstention. The M1 forming bar is NOT a completed candlestick; no intrabar OHLC sequencing or execution price is claimed.

The subminute engine computes direction-correct bid/ask *hypothetical* side-mark P&L from actual fill/lot and original intent stop, using only the **pre-open frozen fee reserve** if it was verified. Two causal quotes at least 5 and at most 35 seconds apart are necessary for an actual direction-change signal:
- `FAST_STILL_POSITIVE_NET_FADE_WATCH` only when both as-of quote marks exceeded independently estimated commissions, the second declined >=0.07 original-stop-risk units, and the observed estimated net was decreasing but **still positive**.
- `FAST_APPROACHING_ORIGINAL_STOP_WATCH` when price was within 0.35 original stop-risk units from stop and the latest observed move deteriorated at least 0.045R. This may have no positive net profit and is NOT an invitation to increase stop distance.
- Otherwise `NO_STRONG_SUBMINUTE_WARNING` or `NO_VALID_SUBMINUTE_DIRECTION_COMPARISON`. No probabilities, no broker stop changes, no new trade gate, no automatic close.

A new, independently frozen `subminute-quote-activation.json` ties first eligible sub-minute observations to exact owner/account hashes, DEMO/USD account scope, source and rule parameters, and explicitly forbids automatic closure, stop amendment or model promotion. Older trades and those already closed before activation are not retrospectively scored. The independently reviewed 16:36 USDJPY loss is **not** a prospective successful prediction by this model.

Observer state and append-only event journal live in the existing isolated `irexpro-research/recovery-risk-forward` directory, plus `rapidQuoteForwardValidation` scoreboard in existing `latest-summary.json`. This scoreboard counts unique trades, first-minute closes with and without actual pre-close quote evidence, warned positions that eventually won (false-alert danger), and actual settled after-cost P&L without repeating a trade's money for each quote. Existing severe warning, early positive-profit caution and 5/10/15/20-minute outcome tracking stay unchanged. No broker API call, trading service restart, or owner demo risk configuration modification is required.
